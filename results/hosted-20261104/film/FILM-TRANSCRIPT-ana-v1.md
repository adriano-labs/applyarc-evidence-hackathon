- **0:00.0** Adrian: I'm Adrian, founder of ApplyArc, a UK job-search startup that existed before this hackathon. Everything here was built during it, on Meta's models.
- **0:10.0** Adrian: A real council job advert says: "Using AI? Please follow our guidance to ensure your application reflects your own knowledge and experience."
- **0:19.1** Adrian: Ana is fictional. Her problem is real. Three years in a Leeds care home, working English every day, and a form that wants written English.
- **0:28.2** Adrian: She says it in Portuguese.
- **0:30.9** Ana (synthetic voice): Ana (synthetic voice, in Portuguese): “Chamo-me Ana. Eu trabalho há três anos num lar em Leeds…”
- **0:37.5** Adrian: Muse Voice Transcribe writes it down, live, keeping her English care words in English. She checks her words first.
- **0:45.3** Adrian: Muse Wonder turns her words into facts. Each one quotes her exact Portuguese. Code checks it, word for word. The English meaning sits underneath. She confirms both.
- **0:56.6** Adrian: Wonder checks the council's ten points and finds the one she didn't cover: dignity and respect. It asks in Portuguese.
- **1:04.0** Ana (synthetic voice): Ana (synthetic voice, in Portuguese): “Para mim, dignidade é bater à porta antes de entrar…”
- **1:13.5** Adrian: Wonder drafts her supporting evidence and a short letter, in English. Every factual sentence cites a fact she confirmed, and a second Wonder call checks it against her Portuguese.
- **1:25.4** Adrian: Then she remembers: only two of her three years were nights. She says it once.
- **1:33.2** Ana (synthetic voice): Ana (synthetic voice, in Portuguese): “Correção: os turnos da noite foram dois anos, não três…”
- **1:41.7** Adrian: Wonder spots the contradiction and asks which is right. She picks the correction. Only the sentences that cited the old fact reopen, in both documents.
- **1:58.1** Adrian: Nothing gets submitted for her: the pack is stamped NOT SUBMITTED. The employer gets one page where every factual sentence opens her Portuguese words.
- **2:09.7** Adrian: What Meta did, step by step: model, host and Meta's request ids. Both models are weeks old. A verifier re-checks the record without calling a model.
- **2:21.9** Adrian: The same loop runs on Meta's open weights, Muse Spark Open, on Modal, so a care provider chooses where it runs.
- **2:31.6** Adrian: That's ApplyArc Evidence. Who pays? Councils and care employers who need applications in the applicant's own words. It never screens, ranks or submits anything.
