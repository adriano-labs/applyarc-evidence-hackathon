# Comparison protocol v5, declared before the final runs (29 Oct 2026, 19:46Z)

Set 2's results stand as recorded under v4; nothing is regraded. v5 changes only how the harness drives the app arm, plus the set 3B steps in `ACCEPTANCE-SET3B.md`.

**The defect it fixes.** Under v4 the app arm answered a question only if it sat on the form's first requirement (`own-answer-1`) and skipped every other open question, then sent the case's fixed answer as notes. When the app asked its contribution question on another requirement, the answer arrived unlinked to that requirement. Exposure in set 2: 5 Baseten cases, 1 Meta, 5 Modal; these are exposure counts, not proven losses. The baseline arm received the same text directly and was not affected.

**v5.** The case's fixed clarification answer goes to the app's open question on the first requirement the prepared form marks as asking what the applicant did themselves; if no such question is open, it is sent as notes, as in v4. Every other open question is skipped, and each skip is recorded with its requirement. The baseline arm is unchanged. The fixed text both arms receive is unchanged.

**Proof before use.** A harness unit test that fails under v4 and passes under v5, on a seen case; then the frozen evaluation SHA, then the final runs. The protocol label in every report says v5.
