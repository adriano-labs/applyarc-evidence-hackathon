Six years at Fernhill Home in Leeds. Started on the tills in 2020, store supervisor from 2023, assistant manager since March 2025.

Marketing bits: I set up the store's Instagram in 2024 and ran it myself, posting three times a week. It went from 300 to 2,100 followers in eight months.

We ran a meet the makers Saturday with local suppliers in May 2025. Around 150 people came. Footfall that month was up 12% on the year before, according to the head office report.

Window displays: our team of four changed the windows every month. I planned the themes and kept to the £200 monthly budget.

Loyalty emails: head office sends them, but for a year I wrote the local events section for our store's version.

Problem: for the first two months on Instagram nothing happened, about 10 likes a post. I looked at what other local shops posted and switched from product photos to short videos of the team setting up displays. Likes per post roughly tripled.

Course: Google Digital Garage, Fundamentals of Digital Marketing, 40 hours, finished June 2026.

Why marketing: I enjoyed the Instagram and the events far more than rotas and stock counts. I want to do it full time, and stay in Leeds.
