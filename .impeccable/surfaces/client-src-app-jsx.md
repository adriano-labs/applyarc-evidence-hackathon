Scope: the whole ApplyArc Evidence workspace (one screen with Form, Document and Evidence areas) plus its export preview. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience: UK graduates finishing real applications; judges on desktop. Task: notes → confirmed facts → one question → two drafts → a changed fact → reviewed changes → approved versions → NOT SUBMITTED export.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An application written like a well-referenced paper: every drafted sentence carries a numbered citation to a fact in the applicant's own words, and a changed fact issues a corrigendum touching only the sentences that cite it. Refuses the chat-bubble AI writer and the card dashboard with a purple glow.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper-white ground, an Oxford-blue shell rail and header, ink black for the applicant's words, pencil grey for drafted text, highlighter yellow for quoted evidence, highlighter pink for "needs you", green for confirmed. STIX Two Text (the journal face) for documents and notes at a 60–70 character measure; system UI sans for controls; tabular numerals. Citations hang in a shoulder margin beside the measure. Nothing vanishes: superseded approvals and rejected facts stay, struck through and stamped.

STORY: Ellie sees her own words become referenced answers, hovers any citation and sees the exact quote light up, changes one fact and trusts the corrigendum, approves version 3, exports a preprint marked NOT SUBMITTED.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Oxford-blue top bar (product name, application, mode badge FIXTURE / STAND-IN / META, ⌘K). Left column 300px: the form question, a requirements checklist that ticks as facts cover it, word limit, portal tasks. Centre: Project answer | Cover letter tabs, the manuscript at 65ch with citation numerals in the shoulder. Right rail 360px: References (numbered facts with quotes) above the notes; becomes the Corrigendum queue during a repair; "What Meta did" drawer at its foot. Primary action bottom of centre column: the next step's verb.

SIGNATURE: the live citation. Hover or focus [3] and reference 3 lifts in the rail while its quote is swept with highlighter yellow in the notes; in a repair every sentence citing the changed reference is struck and replaced inline, numbered in the queue.
